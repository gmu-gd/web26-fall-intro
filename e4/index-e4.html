Exercise 4

I Can Be Your Hero

Description

Design three hero sections for a website about the Emmy Awards. Each hero section should be unique in layout but can reuse type, colors, and images. Each hero section should include the following content:

  • Headline - Television’s Biggest Night
  • Subhead - The Emmy Awards celebrate the shows, performers, and creators that shape the world of television.
  • Button – See the Winners
  • Image – Each design should include an image (or images)

The designs should not use any of the Emmy Awards branding and must use a column structure. You do not need to add a header or any of the other content that might be on the page, you are just designing the hero section.

Requirements

  • 1440px x 800px
  • Includes above content
  • 3 unique layouts

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Look at existing websites for layout inspiration
  • The columns are there to use, not for decoration

Objectives

  • Continue working in Figma
  • Understanding the benefit of columns

Final Submission

  • Turned in on time
  • All requirements met
  • Prototype AND design link submitted on Canvas
  • Prototype linked on your class index
