Exercise 5

Visualizing Responsiveness

Description

Find a website that has a well design mobile and desktop site with reasonable differences in layout between them. Take screenshotsof the full height of the home page for each size, meaning from top to bottom of the page, and taken with your browser at widths of 1440px, 1024px, 768px, and 320px (or the smallest your browser width will go). Arrange the screenshots taken at each width in Figma on an frame that is the appropriate width and height. Make sure the screenshots overlap so that it looks like a complete recreation of the live site.

Requirements

  • Screenshots for the full height of the 4 sizes
  • Images are the appropriate widths (1440px, 1024px, 768px, 320px) and the height is as tall as the homepage is on the web

Things to Keep in Mind

  • What is changes between each width
  • Be precise about aligning your screenshots

Objectives

  • Begin to understand responsive design

Final Submission

  • Turned in on time
  • All requirements met
  • Prototype link submitted on Canvas
  • Linked on your class index
